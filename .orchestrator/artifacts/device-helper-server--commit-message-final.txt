Add an opt-in loopback device helper for screen power and CPU temperature

scripts/device-helper.mjs, enabled with serve.mjs --helper, adds
POST /__helper/display {"on": true|false} and GET /__helper/status
(power strategy, temperature source and tempC). Without --helper the
routes do not exist.

Every route checks, in order, a loopback socket peer, a loopback Host and
the X-Poster-Helper: 1 header; no CORS grant is ever sent and proxy
headers are ignored. Bodies over 1024 bytes get 413, a second request
while a command runs gets 503.

Commands are spawned without a shell from fixed argv arrays (cec-ctl,
cec-client or wlr-randr, auto-detected in that order) with a PATH-only
environment plus the validated compositor variables for wlr-randr. Each
runs in its own detached process group with a 5 s timeout: on timeout the
504 is sent at once and the group is killed; after every command the
group is killed and the lock is held until it is gone (zombie-only groups
count as gone), with a warning after 10 s. Failures return 502.

serve.mjs refuses to start on an invalid helper option or one given
without a value. --helper-fake records calls instead of running them
(GET /__helper/calls) for tests.
