Add frame brightness and a stepped night dim

frameBrightness (10-100 %) dims the frame image with a CSS brightness
filter. With nightDim on, the frame dims to nightDimLevel between
nightDimStart and nightDimEnd. It eases in over the first 10 minutes and
out over the last 10, in whole-minute steps, and a window shorter than
20 minutes peaks below the level. The lower of the manual and night
levels wins. There is no night dim while the clock is implausible.
nightDimTarget 'stage' dims the whole poster instead; the filter is only
ever on #frame or #stage, and the other is cleared.

The filter is never transitioned or animated (Pi GPU). A single timer,
aligned to minute boundaries, sets the value. It is re-evaluated when
settings are applied (live preview, Save and Cancel), when the page
becomes visible, and after a clock jump. The pure effectiveBrightness()
lives in js/brightness.js (precached). The controls join the Display
protection fieldset.

tests/brightness.test.js covers the ramp, midnight, short windows, the
implausible clock and a DST night; tests/e2e/brightness.spec.mjs covers
the filter values, both targets, minute steps and live target switches.
