Show rating, runtime and year, a playback progress bar and the player

With showMeta on, the title card shows the poster's content rating,
runtime and year (e.g. "PG-13 · 2 h 14 m · 1972"), skipping missing
fields; formatRuntime() is exported from js/plex.js. The poster-cache
index stores duration and year, so the overlay also works offline. While
something plays, a 3 px #progress bar along the bottom of the poster
window advances with one linear CSS transition per poll (paused and
buffering freeze it), and with showPlayer on #player-label shows
"Playing in <player>". Toggling either applies at once.

While a household limit is set, each online pool load re-checks the
ratings of every cached title (batched requests of up to 100 keys, at
most 3); any title not confirmed is stored as unrated, so a title Plex
re-rated upward cannot come back from the offline cache. A refused
sessions call (401/403) with now-playing on means nothing is playing and
rotation continues, and a failed playback poll does not trigger a second
sessions request in the same tick.

The mock server reports durations, player names, playback offsets and
state, and answers batched metadata requests. tests/e2e/metadata.spec.mjs
and tests/e2e/now-playing.spec.mjs cover the overlay, the bar and the
label.
