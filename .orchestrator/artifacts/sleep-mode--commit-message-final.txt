Add a sleep schedule and idle sleep that keep the wake lock held

During the sleepStart-sleepEnd window (local time, once the clock looks
set) or after idleSleepHours without playback, the display shows an
opaque black #sleep-veil, sets body.asleep and makes #app inert. The
screen wake lock stays held (D4); no poster is chosen, fetched or drawn
while asleep, including one whose download was already in flight.

While asleep the app still polls /status/sessions through the new
engine.playing() when playback can matter ((sleepEnabled &&
wakeOnPlayback) || idleSleepHours > 0), retrying at the poll rate during
an outage. Playback wakes a scheduled sleep when wakeOnPlayback is on
and always restarts the idle clock. A failed poll is never fatal and
keeps the last playing state for at most 3 failures in a row. One
sessions request is made per tick; decide() reuses it.

A touch or key press wakes the display for 60 s without reaching the
control underneath; clicks stay swallowed for 800 ms after the last
waking press, input aimed at an open dialog is never swallowed, and any
activity restarts the minute. Page load and settings saves restart the
idle clock. A clock step (NTP arriving, a manual change) is absorbed
before every timestamp write so it never reads as idleness or stretches
a wake. Diagnostics shows the sleep state, the local time and "Clock not
set".

js/sleep.js holds the pure decideSleep(); sw.js precaches it.
tests/e2e/sleep.spec.mjs covers the schedule, idle sleep, playback,
manual wake, outages, clock steps and the wake lock.

Phase: sleep-mode (E1, R-PROT-1, D4, D21, D23, D24, D27, D28)
