Add a household content-rating limit that never shows above-limit posters

js/ratings.js maps the US ladder (G = TV-Y = TV-G < PG = TV-Y7 = TV-PG <
PG-13 = TV-14 < R = TV-MA < NC-17) and the country prefixes gb/, de/,
au/, ca/, fr/, nl/ to rungs. allows() is the guarantee: under a limit an
unrated, unmapped or above-limit title is never shown, and an unknown
limit allows nothing. The Plex request lists only allowed ratings and
oversamples the pool (3x, at most 500), but every poster is re-checked
in the app whatever the server returns.

The check covers random posters, the offline cache (entries store their
rating, refreshed when seen online; entries without one are skipped),
the playing title (unless "Apply the limit to what's playing right now"
is off) and the heal re-download. Each draw is re-checked against the
settings in force before and after its decode. Saving a stricter limit
takes an above-limit poster off the screen at once, even offline or
mid-crossfade, replacing it with an allowed cached poster or the empty
card. An owner-saved pin stays exempt; the settings dialog's live
preview never applies an unsaved limit or pin, an imported pin is kept
on Save, and an unknown stored limit becomes PG-13.

Settings gains a "Household with children" toggle (select defaulting to
PG-13). The mock server carries ratings and honours the filter strictly;
tests/e2e/content-rating.spec.mjs covers every route.
