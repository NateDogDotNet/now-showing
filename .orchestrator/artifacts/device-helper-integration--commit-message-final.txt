Power the TV off in sleep and show CPU temperature through the helper

With the new deviceHelper setting on (default off) and the app served
by `serve.mjs --helper` on the device itself, the page uses the
loopback device helper. It powers the screen off once the black sleep
veil has been painted, and on wake it powers the screen back on and
waits TV_WAKE_MS (4000 ms, to be measured on hardware) before fetching
the first poster. Wake-on-playback, the schedule end and a manual wake
all take this path, and a poster fetch that was already running when
the wake began also waits. A failed or slow power call never blocks
waking. Diagnostics shows the CPU temperature and its source,
refreshed only while Diagnostics is open.

With the setting off, the page makes no /__helper/ request at all.
The helper is probed at boot, when Settings opens and when Save turns
the setting on. A live preview never changes the setting. Switching it
off at Save while the app has the TV off sends one last power-on, then
stops all helper calls. js/helper.js sends every request same-origin
with X-Poster-Helper: 1, logs each kind of failure once, and is
precached. The service worker never caches /__helper/ responses.

tests/helper.test.js and tests/e2e/helper.spec.mjs (against
--helper-fake) cover the gating, the power order and timing, the
temperature, failures, and switching the setting on and off through
Settings.
