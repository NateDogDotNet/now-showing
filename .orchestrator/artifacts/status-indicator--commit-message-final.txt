Show the status pill only with the controls and add a quiet status dot

The #status pill now shows only while the controls are visible. While
they are idle-hidden it is never touched (0 mutations), and wake()
brings its text up to date before it reappears. When there is something
to report (paused, offline) and the controls are hidden, a 6 px dot at
35 % opacity (#status-dot) shows instead. It moves to the next corner
of the safe area every 5 minutes on its own timer, independent of pixel
shift, so it never sits still. It follows --ui-rotation, and it is
hidden while asleep and when statusIndicator is 'off'. When all is well
and the controls are hidden, nothing is drawn.

tests/e2e/status.spec.mjs covers the pill, the dot's size, opacity,
corner cycle and rotation, the off setting, sleep, and the wake
catch-up.
