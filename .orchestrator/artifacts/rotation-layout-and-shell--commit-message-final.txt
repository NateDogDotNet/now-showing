Rotate the whole UI with the poster and size it to the viewer's screen

With rotateUi on (the default), the stage, status pill, controls, toast and
settings sheet rotate together by --ui-rotation, and the rotated frame takes
the viewer's width and height (--vw / --vh swap at 90 and 270), so nothing
is clipped or pushed off-screen on a portrait-mounted display. The settings
sheet docks to the viewer's right and stays upright; the toast is capped at
92% of the viewer's width. With rotateUi off only the poster rotates, as
before.

tests/e2e/rotation.spec.mjs checks the stage geometry at 0/90/180/270 on
1920x1080 and 1080x1920 (it fails against the 2.0.0 CSS, where the stage
top lands at 304). tests/e2e/ui-rotation.spec.mjs checks the controls,
status pill, sheet and long toasts inside the viewport, including a
short-landscape 844x390 screen at 90 and 270.
