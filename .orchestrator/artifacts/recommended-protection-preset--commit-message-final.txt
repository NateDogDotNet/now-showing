Add one-tap recommended protection and a one-time What's new notice

A "Recommended protection" button in Settings' Display protection
section lists exactly what it will change, each with a checkbox:
sleep from 01:00 to 07:00, night dim at 60 % on the whole picture
(shown but not untickable), pixel shift and the daily reload. Apply
fills the ticked values into the form and only Save stores them;
Cancel changes nothing. It never turns on idle sleep and never touches
the household content limit, the connection or any other setting. The
preset values live in js/settings-ui.js.

A display upgraded from the previous version and already configured
shows "What's new in 2.1" once, with Open settings taking it (through the kiosk PIN gate)
to Display protection. A fresh install never shows it. The marker
plexPoster.whatsNew is written on the first boot either way.

Also: the Control labels options read "Auto", "Always" and "Never
(icons only)", and the smoke spec pauses its fake clock before the
runFor check.

tests/e2e/preset.spec.mjs covers the preset and the notice.
