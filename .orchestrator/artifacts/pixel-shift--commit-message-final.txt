Move the stage a few pixels every few minutes with a 1.5 s glide

With pixelShift on (the default), the stage keeps a 1.5 % border on each
side (so it is about 3 % smaller on the limiting axis) and moves to the
next point of a 3x3 grid every pixelShiftMinutes. It never stays on the
same point twice in a row, and stage and frame stay inside the viewport
at every rotation. The offset uses the separate CSS translate property,
so it is in screen space whatever the rotation. Each step glides over
1.5 s (ease-in-out) instead of jumping or drifting, and with reduced
motion it moves without the glide. With pixelShift off the layout is
full-bleed as before.

stageSize() takes a margin and the new shiftOffset() gives each step's
offset (js/layout.js). js/display.js runs the step timer and re-syncs it
when settings are saved. A Display protection fieldset in Settings holds
the pixelShift checkbox and pixelShiftMinutes.

tests/layout.test.js and tests/e2e/pixel-shift.spec.mjs cover the
margin, the path, the bounds at both screen shapes and rotations, the
glide, reduced motion and the off setting.
