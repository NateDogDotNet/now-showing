Posters above the limit are never shown, online or from the offline cache, and unrated titles are hidden too. The exceptions are a poster you pinned and saved, and the title playing right now when "Apply the limit to what's playing right now" is off.

Frame & layout
Connect to Plex first, then you can position the poster.

Fills the empty sides of a landscape screen: “Dimmed poster backdrop” with a blurred copy of the poster, or “Title and synopsis” in a panel beside it, hidden when the side is too narrow, as on a 4:3 screen. Any large lit area shortens the life of an OLED screen, so both are off by default. A portrait screen shows nothing extra.

Fills the gap inside the frame’s window when the poster does not reach its edges, with a blurred copy (“Dimmed blurred poster”) or one colour from its edge (“Colour matte”). A gap of a few pixels is closed by scaling or cropping the poster slightly.

Device
Display protection

Sleep at night, dim the picture, shift it a little and reload once a day. You see the list first and can untick anything.

This will change

    Nothing is saved until you press Save. Idle sleep, the content limit and the connection are never touched.

    The picture glides to a new spot inside a thin margin, so no pixel stays lit for hours. Turning it off fills the screen edge to edge.

    At night the dimming eases in over ten minutes and out again before the end, one step a minute. The lower of the two brightness values applies. It waits until the clock is set.

    Gives a display that runs for weeks a fresh start (and after a long sleep, if it has been 12 hours). It only happens when the page is controlled by its offline copy (https or localhost) and the server answers, never while Settings or Diagnostics is open; otherwise it is skipped and Diagnostics says why.

    Only works when the app is served by serve.mjs --helper on the device itself. Off by default: it lets this page run a screen-power command on the device.

    Sleep

    Asleep is a black screen that keeps the display awake, so playback can wake it. With idle sleep the screen stays black between movies. Touch or press a key to wake it for a minute. Turn off your system's own screen blanking.

    Kiosk

    In kiosk mode the controls never appear on a touch, click or key press. Hold a finger or the mouse in the bottom-right corner for 3 seconds, or hold Enter for 3 seconds, to show them for 15 seconds.

    Settings PIN: not set

    The PIN is a deterrent, not security: it keeps casual hands out of Settings, Diagnostics, import, reset and the token. It is stored on this device, so anyone who controls the device can get around it. It is never exported.
